SAMEHEADS — FOR THE NIGHT
Twenty years, 80 things on a dial.

WHAT IS IN HERE
  index.html              the dial page. Open it and tap the wheel.
  assets/                 everything the page needs. Keep the folders together.
  poem-en.mp3             the poem, read in English
  poem-de.mp3             the poem, read in German

TO PUT IT ONLINE
  Upload the whole folder to any web host. index.html must sit at the root.
  It is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript with no build step and no dependencies
  except the fonts in assets/fonts. Nothing needs installing.

The audio is her own reading, recorded on a phone and levelled for phone
speakers. It plays inside the page; nothing is uploaded anywhere.
